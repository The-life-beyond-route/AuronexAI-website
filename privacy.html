Privacy notice · Updated 9 November 2026

What this website collects

When you submit the enquiry form, it collects your name, business name, email address, selected service, message, and confirmation that you agree to be contacted about the enquiry. A reference ID and submission time are also recorded.

How the information is used

The information is used to review your request and respond about potential services. Submitting this form does not subscribe you to a marketing list.

Where it is stored

The included website server stores enquiries in a file on the machine running it. The form does not automatically send your information to an email provider, CRM, or AI service. The website operator is responsible for securing that machine, restricting access, and removing records when they are no longer needed.

Cookies and third-party resources

This version uses no analytics, advertising cookies, or external fonts. Short-lived, in-memory request counters help limit repeated form submissions. Any future hosting, analytics, or integration changes should be reflected in this notice.

Your choices

Only provide information needed to describe your enquiry. Avoid sharing passwords, payment details, health records, or other sensitive information. To request access, correction, or deletion, contact AuronexAI through the enquiry form using the same email address and describe your request.

Before public launch

The business operator should add their verified business identity, direct privacy contact, and retention period, and review this notice against the actual hosting and integrations in use.
