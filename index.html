THE QUESTIONS ON YOUR MIND

Let’s clear
a few things up.

New to automation? You’re in the right place.

Do I need to understand AI to get started?

No. Start with a task that takes too much time. We’ll explain the options in plain language and show you how the finished workflow works.

Can you work with the tools I already use?

That’s the starting point. We check your existing tools, API access, and subscription limitations during discovery. Any changes or additional costs are agreed before work begins.

Will automation replace my team?

Our focus is reducing repetitive admin. We design approvals and human handoffs around the decisions and customer conversations that need your team.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on the workflow, integrations, and access required. After discovery, you receive a scoped plan with milestones and a delivery estimate.

What happens to my business data?

We agree which tools process your data and what access the workflow needs before building. Data handling, retention, and any third-party AI processing should be documented in the project scope.

Can I start with just one automation?

Absolutely. A single, well-defined workflow is often the best place to start. You can expand once you understand how it fits your business.
